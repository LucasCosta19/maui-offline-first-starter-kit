FAQ

Common questions before and after purchase.

Concise answers about the product, licensing, platforms, source access and support scope.

Is this only a UI template?

No. The core value is the offline-first infrastructure: SQLite persistence, durable Outbox, Push/Pull/Bootstrap synchronization, idempotency, concurrency, conflicts, tombstones, authentication and diagnostics.

Do I receive the source code?

Yes. The product is delivered as source code together with documentation, the reference application, the ASP.NET Core API and the automated test suite.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. Commercial apps, SaaS products, internal systems and client projects are allowed under the purchased commercial license.

Can I modify the source?

Yes. You may adapt and extend the source for End Products, subject to the included commercial license and redistribution restrictions.

Can I deliver source to a client?

Yes, when it is privately delivered as part of the specific End Product. A client may maintain that product. General reuse of the Starter Kit for unrelated projects requires its own license.

Developer vs Team?

Developer covers 1 developer with access to the Starter Kit source. Team covers up to 5 developers. End users and people without source access do not consume seats.

Which platforms are validated?

Windows and Android are functionally validated in v1.0.0. iOS and Mac Catalyst source targets are maintained and compile-checked, but not functionally certified in this release.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Ordinary business CRUD is persisted locally first. Synchronization is a separate concern that runs when connectivity and the API are available.

Does it handle conflicts and deletes?

Yes. Conflicts are persisted and resolvable, and server tombstones propagate deletes across clients.

Guaranteed background sync?

No. v1 does not promise guaranteed background synchronization on every mobile platform. Foreground orchestration is the validated baseline.

Attachments, media, push or realtime?

Not in v1.0.0. These are intentionally outside the first release scope.

Hosted backend included?

No hosted service is operated for you. The ASP.NET Core API source is included for deployment in your own environment.

How many tests?

v1.0.0 includes 375 automated tests across Core, Infrastructure and API projects.

What support is included?

The commercial license defines a 12-month Technical Validity Period from the Purchase Date with reasonable technical assistance relating to the documented functioning of the purchased version. Custom development, project implementation, infrastructure administration and SLA support are not included unless separately agreed.

Can I resell the Starter Kit?

No. You may build End Products with it, but you may not redistribute the Starter Kit itself, publish the source publicly, or sell a renamed/substantially equivalent competing starter or template.

Lifetime updates?

No lifetime-update promise is made. The purchased version remains licensed under its terms; access to future versions depends on the offer available at that time.
